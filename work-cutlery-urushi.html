Everyday Cutlery — lacquer + oil

매일의 커트러리 (옻칠+오일)

People in Asia have been lacquering tools and objects with urushi since ancient times. Very old lacquered pieces still turn up in the ground — that’s how long urushi lasts, and how deep its color goes.

I sanded and lacquered the surface that meets the lips more than eight times over. That kind of care doesn’t announce itself. You notice it as you use it.

Details

About 4.2 × 22 × 1 cm. Korean birch. Urushi lacquer where it meets the lips, oil on the handle.

As each piece is carved by hand, dimensions may vary. Slight differences in colour and form are the character of each individual wood.
