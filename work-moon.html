Moon Spoon

달 스푼

I once saw a full moon hanging over the Pacific. Something of it stayed with me, and became this spoon, which you can use from either end.

Porridge, yogurt, ice cream in summer; a scoop for coffee or tea leaves. Each one is carved from Korean birch, so the grain and the color are never twice the same. I hope it brings a small pleasure to your days.

Details

About 3.5 × 14.5 × 1 cm. Korean birch. Walnut oil.

As each piece is carved by hand, dimensions may vary. Slight differences in colour and form are the character of each individual wood.
