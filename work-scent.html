Making · Works

A Place for Scent

향의 자리

Slag — what’s left over when iron is smelted — usually just gets thrown away. There’s a good deal of it on the hill behind my studio.

I coated a piece in white urushi. Under the lacquer the surface hardens and the shape comes forward, the way a scent comes forward with no shape at all. Stand a stick of incense in it, or just let it sit there on its own.

Details

From 5 × 4 × 2.5 cm, following the material. Iron slag (smelting by-product). White urushi lacquer.

As each piece is carved by hand, dimensions may vary. Slight differences in colour and form are the character of each individual wood.
