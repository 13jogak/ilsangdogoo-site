Making · Tools

Leaf

잎사귀

Can green hold yellow inside it? Can yellow promise the green to come? Those were the questions in my head the day I carved these, with a ginkgo leaf in front of me.

I started making them for myself. Now I’d like them to travel like seeds on a spring wind, all the way to you. Each one is cut by hand, so no two grains or edges match — the leaf that reaches you is the only one there is.

Details

About 7 × 13.5 × 1.5 cm (average; varies leaf by leaf). Korean birch, zelkova, cherry, Maackia, toon, persimmon, hackberry and others. Oil or urushi lacquer.

As each piece is carved by hand, dimensions may vary. Slight differences in colour and form are the character of each individual wood.
